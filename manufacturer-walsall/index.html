The stages, key facts and terms to know

The service may cover design, manufacture and installation of the wardrobe, its doors and a fitted interior. The chosen design sets the scope, so clarify the details before approving a plan.

  • Timing: A project moves through design, making and installation, but the time needed varies with the specification and the schedule agreed for the job. Ask for an estimated timetable that names each stage rather than relying on a general promise.
  • Cost factors: The dimensions, door style and finish, interior arrangement and requirements of the room can influence the work involved. Ask for the proposed scope in writing so you can compare providers on the same basis.
  • Provider checks: Find out whether the same service includes design, manufacture and fitting. Ask how the design accounts for the room's measurements, and confirm which storage features and door choices are part of the plan.
  • Glossary: “Bespoke” means the design is tailored to the room and customer's choices. “Mirrored wardrobe” describes wardrobe doors with a mirror finish. “Installation” is the fitting of the manufactured wardrobe in the home.
