Make an alcove work harder

A local alcove wardrobe Walsall project starts with the shape and size of the recess, then builds storage around it. A fitted design can make use of space beside a chimney breast, beneath a sloping ceiling or in another part of the bedroom that standard furniture may not suit. What the service includes

The process is based on your space and storage needs, rather than a standard cabinet size. Design, manufacture and installation are part of the service. Key facts to bear in mind:

  • What it covers: fitted wardrobe design, made-to-measure manufacture and installation, with options for sliding or mirrored doors and interior storage.
  • Typical timing: timing depends on the design, the manufacturing stage and installation arrangements. Ask about the expected schedule for your particular project.
  • What affects cost: dimensions, the shape of the alcove, door choices, finishes and the interior layout all influence the specification. Ask providers to explain what their quote includes rather than comparing headline figures alone.

When comparing providers, check that they have considered the full recess and any awkward angles, and ask who handles design, manufacture and fitting. Look for a clear description of the proposed interior and materials. A few useful terms: made-to-measure means built to fit stated dimensions; interior means the shelves, rails or drawers behind the doors; sliding doors move along a track rather than swinging out.
