What the service includes and how to assess it

The process starts with understanding the room and the kind of storage you want. Design choices may cover the wardrobe’s dimensions, sliding doors, finishes, and interior layout. The wardrobe is then made to suit the agreed design and installed in your home.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- Coverage: bespoke sliding wardrobes, mirrored wardrobe options, and custom bedroom storage, including fitted interiors.
- Timing: there is no single timeline that applies to every room. Design decisions, manufacturing, and installation scheduling can all affect when the work is completed, so ask for the expected stages for your project.
- Cost factors: dimensions, the number and type of doors, chosen finishes, interior fittings, and the shape of the space can affect the overall cost. Ask providers to explain what their proposals include rather than comparing a headline figure alone.

Useful terms include made-to-measure, meaning designed to fit particular dimensions; sliding doors, which move along tracks rather than swinging out; and fitted interior, the shelves, rails, or other storage arranged inside the wardrobe. When comparing providers, check that measurements, design, manufacture, installation, and any interior features are clearly covered in the discussion.
