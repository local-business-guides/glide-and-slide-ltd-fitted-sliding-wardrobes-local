Key facts, from first discussion to fitting

The service can include the design, manufacture and installation of a made-to-measure wardrobe, sliding doors and a fitted interior. The exact scope depends on what you choose for the room.

  • Timing: There is no single reliable duration for every project. Design discussions, manufacturing and installation are separate stages, and the schedule depends on the specification and arrangements confirmed for your job. Ask for an expected schedule for each stage.
  • What affects cost: Room dimensions, door and finish choices, and the design of the interior can all affect the work involved. Request a specification that sets out what is included so you can compare like with like.
  • How to compare providers: Ask who measures and designs the wardrobe, whether manufacture and installation are included, and how the plan handles sloping ceilings or alcoves. Check that the proposed storage suits your needs, not just the appearance of the doors.
  • Useful terms: “Made-to-measure” means designed to fit stated room dimensions. “Fitted interior” refers to the storage inside the wardrobe, such as shelves or hanging space. “Sliding doors” move across the opening rather than swinging out into the room.
