Before seeking proposals, think about what you want to store, whether you need sliding or mirrored doors, and whether the project should include a fitted interior as well as doors.

The scope, timing and cost factors

The service can range from sliding wardrobe doors to a complete fitted interior. The design can be tailored to an alcove, a sloping ceiling or another awkward layout. Be clear about the result you want before asking providers to outline their work.

Key facts for comparing options:
- What it covers: establish whether the proposal includes doors only, internal storage, or a full fitted wardrobe, along with manufacture and installation.
- Typical timing: there is no single timeframe for every design. Ask for the expected schedule across design, manufacture and installation before you make plans.
- What affects cost: room dimensions, the extent of the fitted interior, door choices and the work involved in installation can affect the overall cost. Get a clear breakdown rather than comparing figures with different inclusions.
- How to compare: check that each provider has considered the same storage needs and room layout, then compare the scope and schedule alongside the overall figure.

You may hear alcove, meaning a recess or inset area in a room, and made-to-measure, meaning the design is planned for your particular dimensions. Sliding doors run along a track, while a fitted interior describes the storage behind them.
